Localabs

An iPhone app that turns a photo of a lab report into a plain-language explanation, with a medical language model running entirely on the phone:

  • Scans multi-page reports and reads them with Apple's on-device OCR
  • MedGemma 4B writes a summary, a glossary, diet notes, questions for your next visit and a list of the medications on the report
  • Turns those medications into a daily reminder list
  • Lets you circle any part of the scan and ask about it
  • No account and no cloud

Why I built it

Lab results are hard to interpret, especially when no one at home has medical training. A general-purpose chatbot can hallucinate, so it shouldn't be relied on for this, and it won't remind you about the medications on your report or suggest questions for your follow-up visit.

The hard part

The main challenge was keeping everything on the phone, including iPhones with less memory. With the 2.5 GB model loaded, processing a full 12-megapixel photo pushed 6 GB iPhones past their memory limit, and iOS shut the app down mid-scan. I fixed this by downscaling each photo to 2048 px before OCR. The app also checks for at least 7 GB of RAM at launch, because the App Store can't restrict downloads by device memory.
