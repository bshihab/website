Localabs

An iPhone app that turns a photo of a lab report into a plain-language explanation, with a medical language model running entirely on the phone:

  • scans multi-page reports and reads them with Apple's on-device OCR
  • MedGemma 4B writes a summary, a glossary, diet notes, questions for your next visit and a list of the medications on the report
  • turns those medications into a daily reminder list
  • lets you circle any part of the scan and ask about it
  • no account and no cloud

Why I built it

Reading lab results is hard, especially when no one at home has medical training. Pasting them into a regular chatbot risks it making things up, so it isn't something you should rely on. A chatbot also won't remind you about the medications listed on your report, or give you questions to bring to your follow-up visit.

The hard part

Making everything run on the phone, including phones with less RAM. With the 2.5 GB model loaded, a full 12-megapixel photo pushed 6 GB iPhones past their memory limit, and iOS killed the app at "Scanning…". I got around it by shrinking each photo to 2048 px before OCR, and the app checks for at least 7 GB of RAM at launch, since the App Store can't block downloads by memory.
