Scribend

An offline medical scribe my team built at the Qualcomm × Meta ExecuTorch Hackathon. It records a doctor–patient visit and writes a structured SOAP note on an Android phone, with no internet:

  • Whisper tiny.en for speech to text and Llama 3.2 1B for the note, both through ExecuTorch
  • Ran on a Galaxy S25 Ultra at about 49 tokens a second, on the CPU
  • No network needed after the first model download
  • My part was the on-device models and the Android build; my teammates built the model exports, the patient-history search and the app interface

Why I built it

Clinics in remote areas often lack reliable internet, so a cloud-based scribe doesn't work there. The hackathon focused on running AI on Snapdragon phones with ExecuTorch, which made an offline scribe a natural fit.

The hard part

The biggest constraint was memory. Llama 3.2 3B wrote better notes than the 1B, which sometimes echoed the prompt, but running it alongside Whisper exhausted the phone's memory and crashed the app. After testing the combinations on the S25, I went with Whisper tiny and Llama 3.2 1B, which ran reliably in all three runs at about 49 tokens per second.
