Scribend

An offline medical scribe my team built at the Qualcomm × Meta ExecuTorch Hackathon. It records a doctor–patient visit and writes a structured SOAP note on an Android phone, with no internet:

  • Whisper tiny.en for speech to text and Llama 3.2 1B for the note, both through ExecuTorch
  • ran on a Galaxy S25 Ultra at about 49 tokens a second, on the CPU
  • no network needed after the first model download
  • my part was the on-device models and the Android build; Mariam, Ishani and Bhasky built the model exports, the patient-history search and the app

Why I built it

Clinics in remote areas often don't have reliable internet, and a scribe that depends on the cloud doesn't work there. The hackathon was about running AI on Snapdragon phones with ExecuTorch, so an offline scribe fit.

The hard part

Getting it onto the phone's NPU, which was the point of the hackathon. Qualcomm's QNN toolchain only builds on Linux, so I spent most of the night building it in a Codespace that kept running out of memory. By 1:49 a.m. I had Whisper exported for the phone's chip but nothing running on it, so at 2:48 I switched to the CPU. Llama 3.2 1B wrote its first SOAP note on the S25 at 5:05 a.m.
